Plate 03—Methods

Four transmutations

Nothing on the shelf is poured straight from a bottle. Every drink passes through at least one of these four processes before it reaches the glass.

  1. I

    Clarify

    Citrus juice is cloudy and fades within an hour. We split it with a little milk or agar, let the curds fall, and filter slowly through paper. What comes out is bright, clear and steady for days.

    12 h4 °CPaper, patience

  2. II

    Infuse

    Leaves and roots go under vacuum with the liquid. When the pressure returns, flavour is pushed into the drink in minutes rather than days, so herbs taste fresh instead of stewed.

    10 min−0.9 barChamber sealer

  3. III

    Ferment

    Kombucha, water kefir and koji are living cultures. We feed them, wait, and taste every morning. Fermentation adds a soft natural fizz and a savoury depth that sugar alone cannot.

    2–10 days22 °CAirlocks, notebooks

  4. IV

    Carbonate

    Chilled to just above freezing, the finished drink is charged with carbon dioxide in a steel keg or bottle. Colder liquid holds more gas, so the bubbles stay fine and last to the final sip.

    Overnight−1 °C3.5 bar

      Plate 05—The studio

      A drink is an experiment you get to finish.

      The Alchemist is a bench, a batch log and a small bar in one room. We work like a test kitchen: every idea gets a number, every number gets tasted, and most of them never leave the notebook.

      1. 01

        Start with a question

        “What does smoke taste like when it is cold?” comes before any recipe. The drink is the answer we can pour.

      2. 02

        Zero-proof gets equal time

        Spirit-free drinks go through the same clarifying, fermenting and testing as everything else. They are not an afterthought on the back page.

      3. 03

        Waste is an ingredient

        Squeezed citrus becomes cordial. Spent tea becomes smoke. Peach skins become syrup. The bin is the last stop, not the first.

      4. 04

        Write everything down

        Every batch has a number, a date and a tasting note. When a drink works, we can make it again. When it fails, we know why.
